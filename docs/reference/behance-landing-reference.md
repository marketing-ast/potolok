Reusable observations for Bakha Potolok:

- Product category is the first screen: натяжные потолки / монтаж.
- Hero should feel like a renovation service, not a generic agency page.
- Strong contrast helps: clean ceiling photo, direct headline, phone number, primary CTA.
- Service pages in this segment work better with short claims: speed, clean work, price before installation, warranty.
- A semi-visual process block is useful because clients want to know what happens after a call.
- Legal and contact information should be easy to find in the footer.

Chosen direction for this project:

- Original static landing with light paper background, graphite text, warm yellow construction accent, and generated hero image.
- Copy written for middle/econom segment in Kazakhstan: clear value, affordable price logic, safe materials, quick work.
- Separate legal pages: privacy policy and personal data processing.
